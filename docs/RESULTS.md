# Results

Every record below was produced by `python3 -m opencif build <spec>`, and `code` is the hash of the package source and data that built it, which all rows should share. The columns are the exit code (0 PASS/REPRODUCED, 2 FAIL, 3 outside the validity envelope, 4 INCOMPLETE, 5 REFUSED); N gaps (NM in the head); the target D; the undeclared gaps outside D ± 1%; the share of the net filtrate extracted within D ± 1%; F, target and drawn; Q1 (the largest difference between the drawn and the intended cutoff, in units of D); the declared departures for each option; the head asked for and what was drawn (the arms and their lane pairs, the unit widths, and gap 1 sealed or declared); the no-intersection check (every part at least the floor from every part that it is not joined to) and the check that the drawn components are those requested; the junction model band (the rows that leave D ± 1% when the bend, head T-junction, and outlet stretch-end terms take either end of their range, and the largest change in units of D); the validity-envelope numbers (u_min = min D/w_c against the plateau 0.035, the gap dominance M13 against 0.05, the channel Re against 10, and the larger of the Stokes numbers Re_gap and Re_delta against 1e-2); the outlet balance (the rows that leave D ± 1% when both side outlets' resistance moves by ±1%, the largest change in units of D, and those rows' share of F, docs/LIMITATIONS.md item 15a); the depth over the narrowest drawn CIF channel (measured, since rc5, over gap openings, the center channel, side channels, U-turn sides, head units, and outlet leads, which is a gate in strict mode and is reported in the other modes, docs/LIMITATIONS.md item 7); and the fabrication sensitivity on the design network (validation.robustness_design_network). The bias and noise columns give the gaps outside D ± 1% under a uniform width bias of +0.5 and -0.5 µm on every lateral width (and their share of N), with the range of the cutoff over D across both biases (row 1 and the inner rows) and the gaps that lose their cutoff (reverse flow or f >= 1/2; a seam row), and the gaps outside D ± 1% for one draw (seed 1) of 10 nm random per-segment side-width noise. The ± 1% columns are statements about the nominal geometry, whereas these two say how far a real chip departs from it (docs/LIMITATIONS.md items 13-14).
